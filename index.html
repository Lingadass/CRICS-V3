CRICS - Offline Cricket Scoring App

CRICS provides ball-by-ball offline cricket scoring, player registration with ID cards and QR passes, career statistics, tournaments with knockouts, scorecard PDF and image exports, and backup and restore.

Features

  • Ball-by-ball offline scoring
  • Player registry with ID cards and QR passes
  • Career statistics
  • Tournaments with knockout brackets
  • Scorecard PDF and image export
  • Backup and restore
  • English, Hindi and Spanish

Install CRICS from Android Chrome using “Add to Home screen”.
