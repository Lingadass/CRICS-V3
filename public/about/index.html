About CRICS Offline Cricket Scoring

CRICS is a web app for recording cricket matches without an internet connection. It stores match data on the user's device and works as an installable app on supported Android and desktop browsers.

Designed for the scoring workflow

Use the match wizard to set teams and players, record each delivery in the live scorer, and review innings, batting and bowling totals, charts and career statistics.

Created by LINGADASS K

CRICS is produced by eSportZignal and created by LINGADASS K. Your records can be exported as a portable backup and restored later.
