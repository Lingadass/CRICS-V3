CRICS Features for Offline Cricket Scoring

Ball-by-ball match scoring

Record runs, extras, wickets, bowler changes and innings changes. Edit recorded balls or undo and redo deliveries when needed.

Players and QR passes

Maintain a player registry, create ID cards and generate QR passes that can be scanned within the app.

Career statistics and scorecards

Review career batting and bowling statistics and export match scorecards as PDF or images.

Tournaments and knockouts

Manage tournament groups, standings, NRR, points and knockout brackets.

Backup and restore

Export CRICS data as JSON and import it on the same or another supported browser with merge or replace options.

Languages

The app interface is available in English, Hindi and Spanish.
