CRICS Cricket Scorer — Privacy Policy
Effective Date: November 15, 2026
Package ID: com.crics.offline.scorer

1. Summary: All Data Stays on Your Device
CRICS Cricket Scorer operates 100% offline. All data you create or import—including player names, player photos, teams, match scorecards, ball-by-ball records, career statistics, tournament tables, and settings—stays on your device inside your browser or app's local storage (IndexedDB).

2. No Data Collection, No Accounts, and No Network Calls
- We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information or usage data.
- There are no user accounts, sign-ups, cloud databases, advertising SDKs, or analytics trackers.
- The app makes zero network calls at runtime.

3. Optional Scorer Lock PIN
If you set a 4-digit Scorer Lock PIN in Settings, it is stored locally on your device as a salted SHA-256 hash. It is a convenience lock to prevent accidental taps while scoring and is not encryption. By default, exported backup files exclude your PIN unless you explicitly choose to include it.

4. Backups, Exports, and Deleting Your Data
You are in full control of your data at all times:
- You can export your records to a JSON backup file, CSV file, PDF scorecard, or image directly on your device.
- You can delete individual players, teams, matches, or tournaments inside the app, or clear all app data at any time through your device or browser settings.

5. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer listed on the CRICS Cricket Scorer Google Play Store listing.
